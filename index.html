About GharMap
GharMap is operated by Piyush & Himanshu, Delhi NCR, India. Contact: +91 93362 97656.
What we collect
When you book a visit or submit a listing, we collect your name and phone number. This is used only to confirm your visit or listing request.
How we use your contact
Your phone number is shared only with our GharMap team to confirm your visit or listing. It is never shown on the map, never shared with third parties.
Third-party services
We use Leaflet and CartoCDN for map tiles, Supabase for data storage, and Google Sheets for listing management.
Your rights
Request deletion of your data by contacting us on WhatsApp at +91 93362 97656 or messaging us directly from the app.
Terms of Use

Verified listings
Every listing on GharMap is physically visited and verified by our team before going live. However, always verify independently before making any payment.
No payments through GharMap
GharMap does not collect rent, deposits, or any payments. Never pay anyone claiming to be GharMap.
Contact sharing
Your phone number is shared only with our GharMap team. It is never displayed publicly or shared with third parties.
Moderation
We reserve the right to remove any listing or block any user at our discretion. For takedown requests contact us on WhatsApp at +91 93362 97656.
